📖 How to Play

Goal

Be first to reach the treasure on tile 100. You need the exact roll to land on it. Ladders ▲ carry you up, snakes ▼ drag you down. The host (or you, in a solo game) picks the finish rule: Bounce back or Exact roll.

Every turn
  • Pick a die, then roll it. You earn salary each turn.
  • Visit the Black Market once per turn: swap places, freeze a rival, bribe a snake, set a checkpoint, plant a serpent trap or a mine. Or gamble on 🃏 Jack of Trader: pick one of three face-down cards, 60% a boon, 40% a curse.
  • Roll a 6 for an extra roll and a power-up of your choice.
Dice

    A special die costs gold each time you roll it; if you can't afford it you roll the Normal die instead.

    Champions
    • 🧑 Human — extra income every turn.
    • 🧙 Wizard — cheaper market, longer ladders.
    • 😈 Demon — steals gold when it passes rivals.
    • 😇 Angel — starts shielded, immune to freeze.
    Online
    • One player picks Create room and shares the 4-letter code or the invite link.
    • Friends pick Join room and type the code. The host presses Start match once 2–5 players are in.
    • During an online match, tap the 💬 Chat button (bottom right) for quick chat, emoji taunts and messages. Taunts pop up over your token for everyone.
    Controls

    Space roll · 1–4 pick a die · S Black Market · Esc close a window. Meme splashes can be skipped with a tap or Space.
